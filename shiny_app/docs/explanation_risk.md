# Risk and Variability Analysis

## Variance Decomposition
\\[
Var(\pi) = Var(Y_{resp}) P_c^2 + Var(P_c) Y_{resp}^2 + 2Cov(Y_{resp}, P_c)
\\]

## Coefficient of Variation
\\[
CV = \frac{\sigma_{NPV}}{E[NPV]}
\\]

## Probability of Loss
\\[
P(NPV < 0) = 1 - P(NPV > 0)
\\]

## Interpretation
These indicators capture both variability and downside risk in profitability, complementing average outcomes.
