# Adoption and Behavioral Economics

## Probability of Adoption
\\[
Pr(Adopt) = \Phi\left(\frac{E[NPV]}{\sigma_{NPV}}\right)
\\]

## Drivers
1. Liquidity constraints  
2. Information asymmetry  
3. Risk aversion

## Learning Effects
As farmers observe benefits, perceived risk \\( \sigma_{NPV} \\) declines, increasing adoption.

## Interpretation
Adoption is shaped by expected returns, liquidity, and behavioral preferences, not just agronomic profitability.
