# Scaling and Spatial Extrapolation

## Targeting Logic
\\[
ROI(x,y) = \frac{E[NPV(x,y)]}{Cost(x,y)}
\\]

## Integration
Combines NPV surfaces with soil, market, and accessibility data to map profitable lime zones.

## Aggregation
\\[
\bar{NPV}_j = \frac{\sum_{i \in j} NPV_i A_i}{\sum_{i \in j} A_i}
\\]

## Interpretation
Spatial targeting enables cost-effective allocation of lime interventions.
